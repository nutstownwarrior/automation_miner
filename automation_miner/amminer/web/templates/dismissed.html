Your standing preferences

These are generalised from the reasons you gave when dismissing things, so some of them will be wrong — a guess about what you meant, made from a sentence you typed in a hurry. Rewrite any of them. Once you do, the wording is yours and no later run will put it back.

A preference can only hide a suggestion. It never ranks one up and never changes any evidence. Switching one off, rewriting it or deleting it brings back everything it was hiding, straight away.
